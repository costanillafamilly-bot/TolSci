FAQs

Who can apply?Incoming Grade 7 and Grade 11 students with a minimum general average of 90 and no grade below 85, who meet the admission requirements of Toledo City Science High School.
What are the requirements?Accomplished application form Report card (Grade 6 or Grade 11) – photocopy PSA birth certificate (photocopy) Certificate of good moral character 2x2 ID pictures (2 pcs) Parent / guardian ID (photocopy)
When is the admission test?March 14, 2026, 8:00 A.M- 12:00 P.M at TOLEDO CITY SCIENCE HIGH SCHOOL
Is there an application fee?No fee
What should I bring on test day? Pencil, ballpen, valid ID, admission slip.
Which strands are offered?STEM, ABM and HUMSS.
What are the Houses?Handasa (yellow), Techne (red), Manthanein (blue) and Scire (green).
Are there clubs and organizations?Yes! Toastmasters, Chess, Robotics, Science Club, Artist Circle, Performing Arts, Campus Ministry and Troubadours.
Who do I contact for questions?Go to the Contact tab or tap "Ask the Admission Office" above.
